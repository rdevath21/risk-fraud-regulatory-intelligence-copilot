PROBLEM BRIEF

The Problem We Solve

⚠

Industry Context

Banking and NBFC teams manage real-time fraud detection, liquidity risk, credit risk, and regulatory reporting (AML, Basel, local regulations). These processes are largely manual today, creating compliance gaps and investigation backlogs.

Current Pain Points

✕

4-8 hours per alert for manual investigation and evidence compilation

✕

Regulatory policies scattered across dozens of documents with no unified search

✕

No audit trail for AI-assisted decisions — regulators demand explainability

✕

SAR filing backlogs: 30-day deadline often at risk due to slow evidence generation

Target Users

🕵

AML Compliance Analyst

Triages alerts daily, investigates suspicious transactions, generates SAR evidence packages. Needs fast access to transaction data + regulatory context.

👤

BSA Officer / Compliance Manager

Reviews investigations, makes SAR filing decisions, reports to board. Needs governed, auditable outputs with regulatory citations.

📊

Risk Auditor

Performs periodic reviews of investigation quality and AI usage. Needs read-only access with full audit trail visibility.

06 / 08
GUARDRAILS & GOVERNANCE

Governed, Trustworthy AI Behavior

AI Safety Guardrails

🔒
Input Validation

Blocks SQL injection (DROP, DELETE, TRUNCATE), enforces 1000-char limit

🎯
Topic Relevance Check

Warns when questions fall outside risk/fraud/compliance domain

📈
Confidence Scoring

HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW based on regulatory citation count in LLM response

📖
RAG Grounding

LLM uses ONLY provided regulatory context. Flags uncertainty explicitly.

⚠
Low Confidence Warning

User warned to "verify against source documents" when citations are sparse

🛠
Graceful Error Handling

Cortex API failures return user-friendly messages, never raw stack traces

Data Governance

RBAC — Role-Based Access

RISK_ANALYST — Investigate, generate evidence, update alerts

RISK_AUDITOR — Read-only access to all data + full audit trail

PII Protection

Dynamic Masking: PII_MASK policy on name, email, phone, address

Semantic Tags: SNOWFLAKE.CORE.SEMANTIC_CATEGORY applied to all PII columns

RISK_AUDITOR role sees: ***MASKED***

Complete Audit Trail

Every AI interaction logged: action type, alert ID, user query, RAG context retrieved, full LLM response, user identity, timestamp

Human-in-the-Loop Policy

Internal risk policy mandates: AI outputs are advisory only. Human investigator must review and approve before SAR filing.
